# Statement Inserts & Direct Mail Guide

## Statement inserts
Statement inserts ride along with the monthly card statement. They reach **every active cardholder** at near-zero marginal cost.

- CTR is lower (2.0–3.0%) than digital channels, but **conversion quality is the highest**: CVR of 18–22% of clicks and ROAS of 5.5–7.0.
- Best for: Large and Mid-Market segments, high-consideration offers (travel, equipment, partner programs).
- Creative must fit the standard insert slot: 1 page, QR code mandatory, short URL optional.
- Track via QR scan + unique short URL per campaign.

## Direct mail
- Use for new-merchant onboarding and win-back of high-LTV dormant cardholders.
- Average response rate 1.0–1.8%; CPA is the highest of all channels (see channel rollup).
- Always include a unique offer code per piece for attribution.

## Shared rules
- Physical pieces must carry the offer expiry date and full terms (see KB-03).
- Production lead time is 3 weeks — plan seasonal mailings against KB-13.
