# Email Marketing Best Practices

## Performance benchmarks (portfolio averages)
- Open rate: 22–28%
- CTR: 3.5–5.5%
- Conversion rate (of clicks): 12–20%
- Unsubscribe rate ceiling: 0.5% per send

## Rules that move the numbers
1. **Subject lines under 50 characters** outperform longer ones by ~15% CTR.
2. **One CTA per email.** Emails with a single button beat multi-CTA emails on CVR.
3. **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am local time.** Monday and Friday sends underperform.
4. **Segment the list.** Sending the same creative to all segments lowers blended CTR by ~20%.
5. **Re-engagement series:** 3 emails over 21 days to cardholders with no login for 45+ days; stop after any login.

## Deliverability
- Suppress hard bounces immediately.
- Keep complaint rate below 0.1%.
- Authenticate with SPF, DKIM and DMARC (see KB-11).

## Measurement
Every email carries campaign_id and a unique click token. CTR = clicks / impressions.
CVR = conversions / clicks. Report both; never report clicks as conversions.
