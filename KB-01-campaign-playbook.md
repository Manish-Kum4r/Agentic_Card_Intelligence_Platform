# Campaign Playbook

## Campaign lifecycle
Every marketing campaign follows five stages: **Plan → Build → Launch → Measure → Optimize**.

1. **Plan** — define the objective (acquisition, engagement, retention, win-back), the target segment, the channel, and the budget cap.
2. **Build** — write the creative, define the offer, and set the tracking parameters (campaign_id on every impression).
3. **Launch** — start with a 10% pilot audience for 7 days before full rollout.
4. **Measure** — track impressions, clicks, conversions, attributed revenue, CTR, CVR, ROAS and CPA daily.
5. **Optimize** — reallocate budget from campaigns with ROAS below 3.0 toward the top-quartile ROAS campaigns.

## Channel selection rules
- **Email** — best for SMB and re-engagement; lowest cost per impression; average CTR 3.5–5.5%.
- **In-App** — best for active users (logged in within 30 days); average CTR 3.0–4.5%; strongest for feature adoption.
- **Statement Insert** — best for Large and Mid-Market segments; lower CTR (2.0–3.0%) but highest conversion quality and ROAS 5.5–7.0.
- **Direct Mail** — best for new-merchant onboarding and win-back; highest CPA, use only for high-LTV segments.

## Guardrails
- Never exceed the monthly budget cap without approval from Marketing Finance.
- Suppress cardholders who opted out of marketing (see KB-11 Compliance).
- Every campaign must carry a measurable conversion event; if it cannot be measured, it does not ship.
