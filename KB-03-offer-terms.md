# Offer Terms & Rewards Catalog

## Standard earn rates
| Merchant category | Base earn rate |
|---|---|
| Fuel | 1.5% |
| Travel (Air + Hotel) | 1.5–1.8% |
| Software & SaaS | 1.0% |
| Advertising | 1.2% |
| Office Supplies | 1.2% |
| Meals & Entertainment | 2.0% |
| Equipment & Machinery | 1.8% |
| All other categories | 0.5–0.8% |

## Promotional boosts (campaign-linked)
- **Q1 SaaS Cashback** — 2x earn on Software & SaaS for 3 months, capped at 25,000 bonus points.
- **Q4 Holiday Spend Drive** — 5% cashback on Advertising and Equipment for October–November.
- **Travel Rewards Boost** — 3x earn on Air + Hotel for Large segment cardholders.
- **Win-Back Dormant Users** — 2,000 bonus points on first transaction after 90+ days inactive.

## Offer rules
1. Bonus earn applies only to transactions tagged with the campaign_id.
2. Caps are per cardholder per campaign; the ledger enforces them automatically.
3. Offers cannot be combined with another bonus-earn promotion on the same transaction.
4. All offer terms must be printed in the campaign creative and in the monthly statement.
