# Seasonal Campaign Calendar

| Window | Theme | Priority categories | Recommended channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | New-year business planning | Software & SaaS, Professional Fees | Email + In-App |
| Mar | Quarter-end budget flush | Equipment, Advertising | Statement insert |
| Apr–Jun | Growth & hiring season | Advertising, Telecom | Email |
| Jul–Sep | Mid-year reviews, renewals | Software & SaaS renewals | In-App + email |
| Oct–Nov | Holiday peak | Travel, Advertising, Equipment | All channels — largest budget |
| Dec | Year-end + win-back | Win-back offers, loyalty reminders | Email + direct mail (high LTV) |

## Planning rules
- Book statement inserts and direct mail **3 weeks** before the window opens.
- Q4 (Oct–Nov) carries ~30% of annual marketing budget.
- Win-back always runs in December — dormant cardholders are most responsive then.
- Every seasonal campaign still needs a pilot (10% audience, 7 days) per KB-01.
