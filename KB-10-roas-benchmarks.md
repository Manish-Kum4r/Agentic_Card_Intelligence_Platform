# ROAS Benchmarks by Channel

ROAS = attributed revenue / campaign budget. Benchmarks are portfolio rolling-12-month medians.

| Channel | Median ROAS | Top-quartile ROAS | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statement Insert | 6.2 | 7.5 | Highest quality conversions; long production lead time |
| In-App | 4.6 | 5.8 | Best for active users; frequency-capped |
| Email | 4.4 | 5.6 | Cheapest scale; creative fatigue is the main limiter |
| Direct Mail | 3.1 | 4.0 | Use only for high-LTV win-back/onboarding |

## Portfolio rules
- **Blended ROAS target: ≥ 4.0.** Below 4.0, shift budget to top-quartile campaigns.
- Any single campaign below **ROAS 3.0 for two consecutive months** is paused and reviewed.
- Win-back campaigns are allowed ROAS as low as 2.0 for one test cycle, because they protect long-term LTV.
- ROAS is attributed revenue, not profit — Marketing Finance applies the margin factor before budget decisions.

## Attribution note
Attribution window is 30 days post-click. Last-click for digital channels; QR/short-URL for inserts and mail. Always state the window when quoting ROAS.
