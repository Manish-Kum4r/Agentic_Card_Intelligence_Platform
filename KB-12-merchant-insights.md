# Merchant Category Insights

## Category landscape (portfolio mix, % of spend)
- Travel (Air + Hotel): ~22%
- Equipment & Machinery: ~14%
- Software & SaaS: ~13%
- Advertising: ~11%
- Fuel: ~10%
- Professional Fees: ~8%
- Telecom, Utilities, Shipping, M&E, Office Supplies: ~22% combined

## What the categories tell you
- **Fuel + Telecom + Utilities** are the "subscription-like" base of SMB spend — stable, low-churn, low insight value.
- **Software & SaaS** spend correlates strongly with engagement: cardholders buying SaaS log in 30% more often.
- **Travel** spend spikes in holiday windows (see KB-13) and is the best target for Q4 campaigns.
- **Advertising** spend by SMBs is a growth signal — these cardholders are investing in their own growth.
- **Equipment & Machinery** has the highest earn-rate sensitivity — cashback on this category moves ROAS most.

## Usage rules
- Category-level insights may be shared with merchants in aggregate (min 50 cardholders per cell).
- Never expose one cardholder's merchant history in any creative or report.
