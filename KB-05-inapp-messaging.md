# In-App Messaging Guide

## When to use In-App
In-App messages reach cardholders inside the mobile app or portal. Use them for:
- Feature adoption nudges (e.g., "set up auto-reports")
- Real-time offer prompts at moments of intent
- Low-urgency reminders (statement ready, points expiring)

Average CTR for In-App is 3.0–4.5%, and conversion quality is the highest of all digital channels.

## Frequency capping (mandatory)
- Maximum **2 In-App messages per cardholder per week**.
- Maximum **1 promotional In-App per day**.
- Never send In-App to a cardholder who has not opened the app in 30+ days — use Email instead.

## Creative rules
- Headline ≤ 40 characters, body ≤ 120 characters, one button.
- Personalize with first name and segment only.
- A/B test the CTA label ("See offer" vs "Get cashback") — label changes alone move CVR by 5–10%.

## Measurement
Track impression, click, dismiss and conversion. Report CTR and CVR; a dismiss rate above 60% means the message is off-target — revise or retire it.
